DataMiner Lite - Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data Collection

DataMiner Lite does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data stays locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage (chrome.storage.local).

Data Stored Locally

The extension stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your Pro/free status and license key (if purchased)
  • Daily usage counters (reset each day)
  • Your API key (if you provide one) - stored only in your browser
  • Extension settings and preferences

API Calls

Data extraction happens locally in your browser. No page content is sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

Stripe is used for payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment transactions. We do not store payment information.

Data Sharing

We do NOT share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties. Period.

Permissions

The extension requests only the minimum permissions needed to function:

  • storage: Save your settings and preferences locally
  • activeTab: Access the current tab when you click the extension
  • scripting: Inject content scripts for page interaction

Questions? Email: support@lightcubelabs.com

Changes
