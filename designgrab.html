Privacy Policy

DesignGrab - Chrome Extension | Last updated: May 31, 2026

About This Extension

DesignGrab lets you instantly capture and extract design assets, colors, fonts, and spacing from any webpage for design inspiration and reference.

Data Collection

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API.

Permissions Justification

  • activeTab - Required to inspect the currently active tab's DOM elements, styles, and assets when you activate the extension.
  • storage - Used to save your captured design assets and extension preferences locally in your browser.
  • clipboardWrite - Allows copying extracted colors, CSS values, and asset data to your clipboard.

Third-Party Services

This extension does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services.

Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
