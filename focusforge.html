Privacy Policy

FocusForge - Chrome Extension | Last updated: May 31, 2026

About This Extension

FocusForge analyzes your browsing patterns to help you understand your focus habits and build productive work sessions with AI-powered insights.

Data Collection

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

Browsing history analysis is performed entirely locally within your browser. No browsing data is sent to any server. AI analysis (when enabled) sends only aggregated, anonymized statistics to process insights.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API.

Permissions Justification

  • tabs - Required to monitor which tabs are active and track time spent on different sites for focus analysis.
  • storage - Used to save your focus session data, productivity scores, and extension preferences locally.
  • alarms - Powers the Pomodoro timer, break reminders, and scheduled focus sessions.
  • history - Enables local-only analysis of browsing patterns to identify productivity trends and distractions.
  • notifications - Sends local browser notifications for focus session starts, breaks, and daily summaries.

AI Analysis

When AI-powered insights are enabled, only aggregated statistics (such as "45 minutes on productivity sites, 12 minutes on social media") are processed. No URLs, page titles, or personally identifiable browsing history is transmitted.

Third-Party Services

AI scoring features, when opted in, communicate with an AI processing endpoint using only anonymized, aggregated data. No personal information or raw browsing history is ever shared.
