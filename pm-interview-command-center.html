Privacy Policy

PM Interview Command Center - Chrome Extension | Last updated: May 31, 2026

About This Extension

PM Interview Command Center is a comprehensive product manager interview preparation tool with structured practice questions, AI-powered answer scoring, and progress tracking.

Data Collection

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

Practice answers are processed locally or via anonymized AI scoring. No personal information is stored on external servers.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API.

Permissions Justification

  • storage - Used to save your practice answers, progress scores, study plans, and extension preferences locally in your browser.
  • unlimitedStorage - Required to store a comprehensive library of interview questions, your accumulated practice history, and detailed scoring data without hitting browser storage limits.
  • notifications - Sends local reminders for scheduled practice sessions and daily study goals.

AI Scoring

When AI answer scoring is used, only the text of your practice answer and the question prompt are sent for evaluation. No identifying information, browser data, or usage patterns are included in AI scoring requests.

Third-Party Services

AI scoring features communicate with an AI processing endpoint using only the practice question and your answer text. No personal metadata, browsing history, or account information is ever transmitted.

Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
